## Executive Summary
Cyclistic is a premier bike-share program in Chicago featuring more than 5,800 bicycles and 600 docking stations[cite: 1]. Financial analysis indicates that annual members are significantly more profitable than casual riders[cite: 1]. Maximizing the number of annual memberships is key to future business growth[cite: 1].

This project analyzes 6,171,756 trip records covering a 12-month period (October 2025 – September 2026) to identify how casual riders and annual members use Cyclistic bikes differently, providing data-driven recommendations to convert casual riders into annual members[cite: 1].

## Key Findings

### 1. Trip Duration
- Casual riders consistently log significantly longer single-trip durations compared to annual members[cite: 1].
- Casual trips average ~1225.2 seconds (~20.4 mins) compared to ~740.5 seconds (~12.3 mins) for members across all days[cite: 1].

### 2. Weekly Patterns
- Annual Members: Activity peaks sharply during traditional weekday commuting hours (Monday through Friday)[cite: 1].
- Casual Riders: Activity concentrates heavily on weekends (Saturday and Sunday) for leisure and recreation[cite: 1].

### 3. Hourly Commute Spikes
- Members: Display pronounced dual-peak spikes at 8:00 AM and 5:00 PM (typical work commute windows)[cite: 1].
- Casual Riders: Volume builds gradually during the day, peaking in the afternoon around 5:00 PM – 6:00 PM[cite: 1].

### 4. Seasonal Trends
- Both user segments experience heightened activity during summer months (June to August)[cite: 1].
- Casual rider demand drops sharply during winter, showing high seasonal sensitivity[cite: 1].

---

## Strategic Recommendations

### 1. Weekend & Leisure Conversion Campaigns
- Launch targeted digital promotions near popular recreational stations, parks, and tourist spots during weekends[cite: 1].
- Offer limited-time "weekend-to-annual" membership discounts for riders who log long weekend rides[cite: 1].

### 2. Commuter Incentives
- Introduce gamified rewards or loyalty points for weekday peak rides to encourage casual weekday riders to upgrade to full memberships[cite: 1].

### 3. Seasonal Membership Passes
- Offer flexible "Spring/Summer Seasonal Passes" or trial bundles starting in April to capture warm-weather casual demand and gradually transition them into full annual memberships[cite: 1].
