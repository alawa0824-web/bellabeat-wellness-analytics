## Executive Summary
Bellabeat is a high-tech manufacturer of health-focused products for women[cite: 7]. This project analyzes public smart device fitness data (FitBit Fitness Tracker Data) to uncover user behavioral patterns regarding daily activity, sleep hygiene, and weight tracking[cite: 7]. The findings inform data-driven marketing strategies and product enhancement initiatives across Bellabeat's ecosystem[cite: 7].

## Key Findings

### 1. Sedentary Lifestyle & Sleep Impact
- A moderate-to-strong negative correlation (-0.53) was established between daytime sedentary minutes and total sleep duration[cite: 7].
- High daytime inactivity directly correlates with reduced overall sleep hygiene and shorter sleep duration[cite: 7].

### 2. Daily Step Distribution
- Users logged an average of 7,281 steps per day, clustering mostly between 4,000 and 10,000 steps[cite: 7].
- A significant portion of users fall short of the recommended 10,000-step daily benchmark[cite: 7].

### 3. Peak Activity Windows
- Hourly step analysis revealed bimodal activity peaks during midday (12:00 PM) and early evening (5:00 PM – 7:00 PM)[cite: 7].
- Activity drops sharply during nighttime hours, bottoming out between 1:00 AM and 5:00 AM[cite: 7].

### 4. Health Tracking Friction
- Weight log entries showed a heavy reliance on manual reporting (64.3%) compared to automatic entries (35.7%)[cite: 7].
- Manual data entry presents a primary user friction point that leads to long-term tracking drop-offs[cite: 7].

---

## Strategic Recommendations

### 1. Targeted Inactivity Alerts
- Program wearable devices (Leaf, Time) and the Bellabeat app to issue gentle vibration prompts during prolonged daytime inactivity to help users improve overall sleep quality[cite: 7].

### 2. Timed Push Notifications
- Schedule activity prompts and wellness reminders immediately before natural peak hours (12:00 PM and 5:00 PM – 7:00 PM) when users are most likely to engage[cite: 7].

### 3. Streamlined Ecosystem Integration
- Highlight automated tracking features (such as smart scales and the Spring water bottle integration) in marketing campaigns to reduce manual tracking burden and improve user retention[cite: 7].
